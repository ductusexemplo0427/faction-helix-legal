Faction Helix — How to Use the Bot

2026-09-21

Faction Helix is a free Discord bot for Marvel Rivals communities. It tracks ranks and stats, organizes teams into factions, runs challenges and leaderboards, and hands out awards — all from stats you report yourself, so there's nothing to link to a game account.

Many servers also add J.A.R.V.I.S., an optional companion bot that hosts hero training sessions, handles support tickets, posts events and Marvel Rivals news, and helps organize faction tournaments. This guide covers both bots.

Getting started

The easiest way in is the menu: type /menu, or just mention the bot (@Faction Helix). This opens a private, multi-page hub with a button for almost everything below — you fill in a small form or pick from a drop-down instead of memorizing slash commands.

Every slash command still works on its own too, and every menu button runs the exact same command behind the scenes, so use whichever feels easier.

Linking your player and reporting stats

Faction Helix doesn't connect to your Marvel Rivals account. Instead you tell it your numbers, and it keeps track of them over time.

CommandWhat it does
/linkConnects your Discord account to an in-game name, so your stats and rank have a home
/report rankReports your current tier, division and points (for example Gold II, 40 points)
/report statsReports matches, wins, kills, deaths and assists
/report heroReports matches, wins and hours on one hero
/favoriteSets a favorite hero (and outfit) to show on your profile card
/refreshUpdates your data after reporting
/unlinkRemoves your linked player and stats

Because everything is self-reported, only report your own real numbers — it's what your factionmates and the leaderboard see.

Ranks, roles and leaderboards

CommandWhat it does
/rankShows your current reported rank
/progressA chart of how your rank has moved over time
/leaderboardThe server's top players
/compareSets your stats side by side with another member's
/serverstatsHow the whole server's ranks and heroes are spread

If an admin has turned on rank roles, you'll automatically get a colored Discord role (Bronze through One Above All) that updates as you report a new rank.

Factions

A faction is a team of up to six members.

CommandWhat it does
/faction createStarts a faction and makes you its leader
/faction joinJoins an open faction, or asks the leader if it's private
/faction leaveLeaves your faction
/faction infoYour faction and its members
/faction listEvery faction in the server
/faction topFactions ranked by average rank
/faction statsYour faction's numbers
/faction styleSets your faction's color and emblem (leaders only)

A faction's leader can also request a private channel and role for the faction (a moderator approves it), and can lock the faction to stop new members from joining freely.

Challenges

Challenges are goals to work toward, alone or with your faction.

CommandWhat it does
/challenge listChallenges you can join
/challenge joinJoins a challenge
/challenge standingsSee how a challenge is going
/challenge historyHow earlier rounds of a challenge went
/challenge fameThe hall of fame — who has won the most rounds
/challenge leaveDrops out and removes your progress

Progress is added with a button on the challenge's card in the menu, or via /challenge submit. Shared goals are challenges the whole group works toward together, with milestone announcements at 25%, 50% and 75%.

Awards and vouching

/awards shows the server's awards and who holds them — admins create and give these by hand to recognize members.

/vouch lets a member vouch for another member's reported stats, which shows on their profile as a light form of verification from someone who has actually played with them.

The menu, in more detail

/menu opens to a Home page with one button per section (You, Rankings, Factions, Challenges, J.A.R.V.I.S. if it's added, Admin for admins, Privacy). Each page lists its buttons with a one-line description, and has Home / previous / next / Close buttons at the bottom.

Everything in the menu is private — only you see it — and every button does exactly what the matching slash command does, so nothing here is a shortcut around the server's rules or an admin's settings.

Meet J.A.R.V.I.S.

J.A.R.V.I.S. is a second, optional bot that hosts and schedules for the server. It's a separate Discord application — its own name, its own permissions — that shares the same data as Faction Helix.

An admin adds it by running /admin jarvis on Faction Helix and pressing Install J.A.R.V.I.S.; only someone with the Manage Server permission can approve it. It's off by default and never asks for the Administrator permission.

Once it's in, an admin turns on the features below with /jarvis <feature> setup, and /jarvis status shows what's on and whether anything needs attention.

Hero training sessions

Hosts (server admins and members with the Coach role) schedule practice sessions for a specific hero.

CommandWhat it does
/session createSchedules a session (title, start time, hero, duration, capacity)
/session listSessions coming up
/session mineSessions you've signed up for
/session cancelCancels a session you host, or any (admins)
/session attendanceWho came, who didn't, and who dropped in (host or admin)

Each session gets a sign-up card with Sign up and Leave buttons, a matching native Discord event tied to a voice channel, a waitlist once it's full, and a reminder an hour before it starts. A session can repeat every week or two.

Tickets: reports, suggestions and support

If a server has tickets turned on, you can privately reach the staff three ways: the staff desk buttons posted in a channel, /ticket open, or right-clicking a message and choosing Apps → Report to staff.

J.A.R.V.I.S. is the middle man: your ticket goes to a staff-only channel, and the staff's replies come back to your direct messages (usually as "the staff team", not a named person). Whatever you reply in DMs is carried back to the staff. You can have one open ticket at a time, and press Close ticket when you're done. Staff use /ticket list, reply, claim and close.

Looking for group

/lfg post posts a card saying what you're after (mode, how many more players, roles or rank wanted, a voice channel) with Join, Leave and Close buttons. When your group fills up, everyone in it is pinged together. A post ends by itself after a few hours if you don't close it first. /lfg list shows what's open right now.

Events, news and announcements

/events lists any Discord event coming up in the server (not just training sessions) — J.A.R.V.I.S. reminds the events channel an hour before and again at the start.

/news shows the latest Marvel Rivals patch notes and news, pulled straight from the game's official Steam announcements. If a server turns this on, new items post automatically as they're published.

/announce lets admins (and anyone with the announcer role) post a formal announcement to the announcements channel.

Faction tournaments

A faction's leader runs /tournament create to set up a tournament page with a countdown and Going / Maybe / Can't go buttons for the whole faction.

/tournament plan suggests up to two team stacks (2 Vanguard, 2 Duelist, 2 Strategist) built from the heroes members have already reported with /report hero. /tournament lineup puts a suggested stack straight onto the roster, or /tournament roster sets one person by hand. /tournament list, /tournament rsvp and /tournament cancel round it out. The page also shows how many practice sessions each roster member attended.

Your data and privacy

Everything is self-reported and stays under your control:

  • /unlink removes your linked player and stats, keeping your Discord account.

  • /deletedata erases everything the bot stores about you — profile, history, faction membership, tickets, sessions, everything — after you type DELETE to confirm.

For the full details on what's collected and why, see the bot's privacy policy — add its link here once it's published.

Getting help

Every feature above can be switched on or off by your server's admins, so if something is missing (rank roles, faction channels, tickets, tournaments), ask an admin — it may simply not be turned on yet in your server.

For a bug, an idea, or a question the bot itself can't answer, ask an admin or moderator directly, or use the ticket system above if the server has it on.
