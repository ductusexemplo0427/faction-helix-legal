Faction Helix Privacy Policy

Effective September 20, 2026

The short version
  • Faction Helix is a Discord bot that shows Marvel Rivals rank cards and server leaderboards.
  • It stores your Discord ID and name, plus the game stats you type in. It does not read your messages, other than noticing when one mentions the bot. If you open a support ticket with the optional J.A.R.V.I.S. bot, what you write in the ticket is passed to the server's staff (see below). J.A.R.V.I.S. also does not read ordinary server messages to run looking-for-group posts, role panels, channel locks, announcements, or tournament pages — those work from button presses and slash commands.
  • Other members of your servers can see your card and leaderboard entry. Server admins can see and manage the information of members in their server.
  • We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • You can delete everything at any time with /deletedata.

2. What the bot does

Members of a Discord server link a player name, enter their own rank and stats, and the bot produces player cards and a server leaderboard. Server admins can also confirm members' reported ranks and manage the information stored for their server.

What we do not collect. The bot does not read or store the content of messages in your servers or your direct messages. The one exception is a message that mentions the bot directly: the bot notices the mention, and whether the rest of the message is empty or says "menu" or "help", so that it can offer its button menu. That text is not stored. The bot does not listen to or record voice channels. J.A.R.V.I.S., the optional second bot from the same operator, only notes who joins the voice channel of a live training session. It also receives direct messages sent to it, but it keeps and forwards one only when you have an open ticket, to pass it to that server's staff; otherwise it ignores what it says. It reads no messages in servers. The server log it can post to a moderators-only channel describes actions (such as a ban, a role change or a new channel) using Discord IDs, mentions and names, never the text of messages, and we do not store those log entries. The bot does not ask for your email address, real name, location, payment details, or game account passwords. We do not run advertising or analytics trackers. The hosting services described below may keep ordinary technical logs (for example, error logs) as part of running the service.

Whether you are a server admin is checked at the moment you run an admin command. We do not store it.

4. How we use it

  • To create your player card, rank, and your server's leaderboard.
  • To let server admins confirm ranks, manage member information, and give awards.
  • To keep the bot working, fix problems, and prevent misuse.

We do not sell or rent your information, use it for advertising or profiling, or use it to train AI models.

5. Who can see your information

  • Other people using the bot. Your card and your leaderboard entry (in-game name, rank, and the stats you reported) are visible to members of the servers where you linked. If you are in a faction, its name is shown on your card in that server, and anyone in the server can see who is in a faction, with their in-game names and ranks, using /faction info and /faction list. Anyone using the bot can also see your rank history as a chart with /progress, compare your stats with theirs using /compare, and see summaries such as member counts, average ranks and popular heroes for a server or faction with /serverstats, /faction stats and /faction top. Anyone using the bot can also ask for your card by mentioning you in /profile, in any server that has the bot.
  • Everyone in a server that uses rank roles. If a server's admin turns on rank roles, the bot gives you a Discord role named after your rank tier (for example Gold) in that server, and everyone in the server can see it. The bot updates or removes it when your rank changes, or when you unlink, delete your data, or are taken off the server's list. To do this, the bot holds the Manage Roles permission in that server. The roles the bot creates carry no permissions.
  • Everyone in a server with the weekly digest or hall of fame. If a server's admin turns on the weekly digest, the bot posts a summary card to a channel each week with the top players, the biggest rank gains, the top factions and challenge results, using players' in-game names and ranks. The challenge hall of fame lists the members who have won the most challenge rounds. Players whose profile is private are left out of both.
  • Everyone who can see a training session. A training session's card, in the channel where it is posted, shows the host and the in-game names (or Discord names) of the people signed up. The host and the server's admins can also see who attended. Attendance is recorded when you join the voice channel of a session while it is live; nothing is recorded from the voice channel itself.
  • Staff, when you open a ticket. A ticket you open with J.A.R.V.I.S. (from the staff desk buttons, /ticket open, or by reporting a message) is posted in a staff-only channel of that server, and its conversation is kept in a thread there. The server's admins and staff role, who read and answer it, can see your Discord name and ID, what you wrote and, for a reported message, a link to it. Your Discord ID and the ticket's number and type can also appear in the server's moderators-only log. J.A.R.V.I.S. delivers the staff's replies to your direct messages, by default as coming from “the staff team” rather than naming the staff member. Other members cannot see your ticket.
  • Moderators, in the server log. If a server's admin turns on the server log, J.A.R.V.I.S. posts cards to a moderators-only channel describing moderation actions, role and channel changes, and (if enabled) members joining and leaving, with the people involved shown by mention. It does not include message text.
  • Everyone in a server with welcomes. If a server's admin turns on welcomes, J.A.R.V.I.S. posts a welcome message that mentions you in a channel when you join, and may give you a starting role.
  • Everyone who can see a looking-for-group post, a role panel, an announcement, or a faction tournament page. These show your Discord name or mention, and, for a tournament, your RSVP and any roster position and hero picks. A faction's tournament page can be posted in that faction's own private channel, visible only to its members and server admins.
  • Everyone who can see a challenge. Challenge standings (your in-game name and progress) are shown in the challenge's channel to everyone who can read it: the whole server for a server challenge, or the faction for a faction challenge. Server admins, and the leader of a faction for that faction's challenges, can see your recent reports and notes and can correct your progress. Corrections are logged.
  • Faction members and moderators. If a faction has a private channel, its members, its leader and the server's admins can read it. When you ask to join such a faction, your Discord name, in-game name and rank are posted for the faction's leader and for the server's moderators, who approve or deny the request. The bot may send you a Discord message with the result.
  • Server admins. Admins of a server where you linked (people with the Manage Server permission) can see that you linked and everything stored about you there, add a player for you, confirm your rank, give you awards, change your rank, stats, heroes and favorite, delete your linked player and its stats, take you out of a faction or close a faction, and take you off their server's list. Each of these actions is recorded in a log that other admins of that server can read, showing which admin acted, what changed, and when. Admins cannot reach information about people who are not on their server's list, and they cannot delete your Discord ID or account record; only you can do that with /deletedata.
  • Us. We can access the database to run and support the service.
  • Service providers. Discord (the platform the bot runs on), Railway (hosts the bot), and Supabase (hosts our database, on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States). They process data on our behalf to provide their services.
  • Game-data service (only if we turn it on). The bot can be switched to a mode that looks up players on a third-party game-data service, MarvelRivalsAPI.com. In that mode, the in-game names you link are sent to that service to fetch your stats. This is not how the bot works today; if we switch to it we will update this policy first.
  • Legal reasons. If the law requires it, or to protect the safety of people or the service.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information until you delete it. You can delete it yourself:

  • /unlink deletes your linked player, reported stats, and rank history. A server admin can also do this for you in their server.
  • /faction leave takes you out of your faction. A faction with no members left is deleted.
  • /deletedata permanently deletes everything above, plus your Discord ID and name, server memberships, faction memberships, join requests, challenge progress and reports, awards, and the admin-action log entries about you. If you led a faction, leadership passes to its longest-standing member. Type DELETE in the confirm box to confirm. This includes your tickets and the messages in them, and your training session sign-ups and attendance.

Tickets that have been closed, with their messages, are deleted automatically 90 days after they are closed; an open ticket is kept until it is closed. A looking-for-group post is removed once it closes or its time runs out. Faction tournament data (the page, roster, and RSVPs) is kept until the tournament finishes, is cancelled, or the faction is disbanded, whichever removes it first. (A copy of a ticket's conversation may remain in the server's staff channel and thread on Discord, which the server's staff control.) Information about a server itself (its ID and name, its settings, and its admin-action log) is kept until the server's admin asks us to delete it. Removing the bot from a server does not delete that server's information automatically. Our database provider may keep backups for a short period after deletion.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See it. /profile shows the card the bot keeps for you. To get a copy of everything we hold, email us.
  • Correct it. Update your stats with /report, or ask a server admin.
  • Delete it. Use /deletedata, or email us.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have additional rights, such as access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection. Contact us to use them, and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Children

You must meet Discord's minimum age to use Discord (13, or higher where local law requires). The bot is not directed at children below that age, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has used the bot, contact us and we will delete the information.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with reputable hosting providers, and access to the database is limited to us. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
